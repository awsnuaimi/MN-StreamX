MN StreamX offline caption engine changes

Whisper uses the original trained full encoder context, original-language
transcription, native cancellation and a per-call deadline. Moonshine and Vosk
are source-specific recognizers; Bergamot performs text translation only.
The Bergamot/Marian source derivative adds Android ABI compatibility, bounded
model metadata reads, and decoder cancellation. The complete modifying source
and reproducible pinned upstream URLs are in app/src/main/cpp. MPL-covered
changes must remain available under MPL-2.0 when distributing the application.
No downloaded model weights are modified by these changes.
